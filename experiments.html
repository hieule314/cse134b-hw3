MathML: mass–energy equivalence

Einstein's equation relates energy to mass:

E = m c 2

Here, E is energy, m is mass, and c is the speed of light in a vacuum.

Being part of the HTML standard did not guarantee that every browser implemented MathML. Firefox supported it for years, but support across browsers was inconsistent. Chrome briefly supported it in version 24, then removed it because of instability; support returned in version 109. Internet Explorer never supported it. This meant developers could not rely on native MathML to display equations for all visitors and often needed images or JavaScript rendering libraries instead.
